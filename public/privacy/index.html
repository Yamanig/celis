Privacy Policy / Siyaasadda Asturnaanta

Last updated: 2026-08-30

This policy explains how Celis Somalia ("Celis", "we", "us") handles information in the Celis buyer and seller marketplace app.

1. Information we collect

Account information includes your email address or phone number used to sign in, password handled by Supabase Auth, display name, avatar, biography, selected region, phone number, seller type, business name, logo, registration number, address, shop slug, seller number, and account dates. Listings include the title, description, price, condition, delivery method, and public photos you upload. We also store buyer-seller conversations and messages, reports, blocks, and a push-notification token when notifications are enabled. We do not collect precise device location.

2. How we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts, publish and manage listings, connect buyers and sellers through Celis messages, deliver requested push notifications, operate seller profiles, prevent abuse, review reports, enforce blocks, and maintain marketplace records. We do not sell personal information.

3. What other people can see

Anyone can see active listings and their public photos. Celis exposes only a limited marketplace identity for attribution: user ID, display name, avatar, verification status, and membership date. Your phone number, email address, push token, business registration number, and business address are not available through the public profile service. Conversation participants can read the messages in their conversation. Reports and block relationships are not public.

4. Service providers and sharing

Supabase processes account authentication, database records, and listing-image storage for Celis. Google Firebase Cloud Messaging processes push tokens and delivery information when push notifications are enabled. We disclose information to these providers only to operate those services. Public listing information is shared with marketplace visitors, and messages are shared with their participants. We do not share data with advertising networks or analytics providers because Celis does not use them.

5. Information kept on your device

Celis keeps your theme, language, preferred region, data-saver choice, saved listing IDs, listing draft, app-tour status, notification choices and permission status, and cached app data in local device storage. These preferences are not uploaded by the app. Removing the app may remove them. Celis uses no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, cross-app tracking, or similar tracking SDK.

6. Security

Celis uses Supabase authentication and database access rules. The profiles and users tables allow signed-in users to read their own rows; public marketplace screens use a separate limited identity service that excludes contact and account-security fields. Conversations and messages are restricted to their participants. Listing photos are intentionally public. No system is completely secure, so report a suspected security or privacy issue to privacy@celis.so.

7. Retention

We keep account and marketplace data while your account is active. Account deletion immediately removes or anonymizes profile, contact, business, push-token, saved-item, notification, block, disposable activity, and untransacted listing data. Existing messages, reviews, moderation reports, completed orders, payouts, and audit records may be retained without your live account identity because another participant, marketplace safety, accounting, or dispute handling may rely on them. Celis has not yet adopted fixed time limits for those retained records; those limits require legal and operational review.

8. Access, correction, export, and deletion

You can review and correct available profile information in the app. To delete your account, open Profile, choose Delete Account, verify your identity, and confirm permanent deletion. Active orders, escrow, wallet payments, or pending payouts must be resolved first. For an access, correction, or portable-copy request that is not available in the app, email privacy@celis.so. We will verify that the requester owns the account before disclosing or changing data.

9. Children's privacy

Celis is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to Celis, contact privacy@celis.so.

10. Location, transfers, and legal rights

Celis is designed for the Somalia market. A location in Celis is a region chosen from a list, not device GPS. Supabase and Google may process data outside Somalia. The governing-law and dispute forum for Celis, and whether laws such as the EU GDPR apply to particular diaspora users, require qualified legal review. Where applicable law gives you additional privacy rights, contact privacy@celis.so to exercise them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Celis features, processors, or legal obligations change. We will change the last-updated date and publish the current policy in the app and at celis.so/privacy. Material changes should be reviewed before release so the policy and Google Play Data safety answers remain consistent.

12. Contact

For privacy requests, account-data questions, or suspected security issues, email privacy@celis.so. Include the email address or phone number linked to your account, but never send your password or one-time verification code.

Markii u dambaysay ee la cusboonaysiiyay: 2026-08-30

Siyaasaddani waxay sharxaysaa sida Celis Somalia ("Celis", "annaga") u maamusho xogta ku jirta abka suuqa iibsadayaasha iyo iibiyayaasha Celis.

1. Xogta aan ururinno

Xogta koontada waxaa ka mid ah emailka ama lambarka telefoonka aad ku gasho, furaha sirta oo Supabase Auth maamusho, magaca muuqda, sawirka astaanta, taariikh-kooban, gobolka aad dooratay, lambarka telefoonka, nooca iibiyaha, magaca iyo astaanta ganacsiga, lambarka diiwaangelinta, cinwaanka, magaca dukaanka ee webka, lambarka iibiyaha, iyo taariikhaha koontada. Xayeysiisyadu waxay ka kooban yihiin cinwaanka, sharaxaadda, qiimaha, xaaladda, habka gaarsiinta, iyo sawirrada dadweynaha ee aad geliso. Waxaan sidoo kale kaydinnaa wada sheekaysiga iyo farriimaha iibsadaha iyo iibiyaha, cabashooyinka, xannibaadaha, iyo calaamadda ogeysiisyada marka aad oggolaato. Ma ururinno goobta saxda ah ee qalabka.

2. Sida aan xogta u isticmaalno

Waxaan xogta u isticmaalnaa samaynta iyo ilaalinta koontooyinka, daabacaadda iyo maamulka xayeysiisyada, isku xirka iibsadayaasha iyo iibiyayaasha iyada oo loo marayo farriimaha Celis, dirista ogeysiisyada aad codsatay, maamulka astaamaha iibiyayaasha, ka hortagga xadgudubka, eegista cabashooyinka, fulinta xannibaadaha, iyo ilaalinta diiwaannada suuqa. Ma iibinno xogta shakhsiga ah.

3. Waxa dadka kale arki karaan

Qof kastaa wuu arki karaa xayeysiisyada firfircoon iyo sawirradooda dadweynaha. Celis wuxuu aqoonsiga suuqa ka muujiyaa oo keliya: aqoonsiga isticmaalaha, magaca muuqda, sawirka astaanta, xaaladda xaqiijinta, iyo taariikhda xubinnimada. Lambarka telefoonka, emailka, calaamadda ogeysiisyada, lambarka diiwaangelinta ganacsiga, iyo cinwaanka ganacsiga lagama heli karo adeegga astaanta dadweynaha. Dadka wada hadalka ku jira ayaa akhrin kara farriimahooda. Cabashooyinka iyo xiriirrada xannibaaddu ma aha kuwo dadweyne.

4. Adeeg-bixiyeyaasha iyo wadaagista

Supabase wuxuu Celis u maamulaa xaqiijinta koontada, diiwaannada kaydka xogta, iyo kaydinta sawirrada xayeysiiska. Google Firebase Cloud Messaging wuxuu maamulaa calaamadaha iyo gudbinta ogeysiisyada marka ogeysiisyadu shidan yihiin. Waxaan xogta siinnaa adeeg-bixiyeyaashan oo keliya si ay adeegyadan u shaqeeyaan. Xogta xayeysiiska dadweynaha waxaa lala wadaagaa booqdayaasha suuqa, farriimahana dadka wada hadalka ku jira. Xog lama wadaagno shabakadaha xayaysiiska ama falanqaynta, sababtoo ah Celis ma isticmaalo.

5. Xogta ku kaydsan qalabkaaga

Celis wuxuu kaydka gudaha ee qalabka ku hayaa midabka, luqadda, gobolka aad dooratay, kaydinta xogta, aqoonsiyada xayeysiisyada aad kaydisay, qabyo-qoraalka xayeysiiska, xaaladda socdaalka abka, doorashooyinka ogeysiisyada iyo oggolaanshahooda, iyo xogta kaydka ku-meelgaarka ah. Abku dookhyadan ma geliyo server-ka. Ka saarista abku way tirtiri kartaa. Celis ma isticmaalo qalab falanqayn, warbixin burbur, xayaysiis, daba-gal ab kale, ama SDK daba-gal la mid ah.

6. Ammaanka

Celis wuxuu isticmaalaa xaqiijinta Supabase iyo xeerarka gelitaanka kaydka xogta. Jadwalka astaamaha iyo isticmaalayaashu qofka galay waxay u oggolaadaan inuu akhriyo safkiisa oo keliya; bogagga dadweynaha ee suuqu waxay adeegsadaan adeeg aqoonsi oo kooban kana reeba xogta xiriirka iyo ammaanka koontada. Wadahadallada iyo farriimaha waxaa arki kara ka qaybgalayaashooda oo keliya. Sawirrada xayeysiisku si ula kac ah ayay dadweyne u yihiin. Nidaam gebi ahaanba ammaan ah ma jiro; arrin ammaan ama asturnaan ah u dir privacy@celis.so.

7. Muddada haynta xogta

Waxaan haynaa xogta koontada iyo suuqa inta koontadu shaqaynayso. Tirtiridda koontadu isla markiiba waxay tirtirtaa ama qarisaa aqoonsiga astaanta, xiriirka, ganacsiga, calaamadda ogeysiisyada, waxyaabaha kaydsan, ogeysiisyada, xannibaadaha, dhaqdhaqaaqa aan loo baahnayn, iyo xayeysiisyada aan macaamil lahayn. Farriimaha hore, qiimaynta, cabashooyinka, dalabyada dhammaystiran, lacag-bixinnada, iyo diiwaannada baaristu waa la hayn karaa iyaga oo aan ku xirnayn koontadaada nool marka qof kale, badbaadada suuqa, xisaabaadka, ama khilaaf xallintu u baahan yihiin. Celis weli ma dejin muddooyin go'an oo diiwaannadan ah; waxay u baahan yihiin dib-u-eegis sharci iyo hawlgal.

8. Helid, sixid, dhoofin, iyo tirtirid

Waxaad abka ka eegi oo ka sixi kartaa xogta astaanta ee la heli karo. Si aad koontada u tirtirto, fur Astaanta, dooro Tirtir Koontada, xaqiiji aqoonsigaaga, kadibna xaqiiji tirtiridda joogtada ah. Dalabyada firfircoon, escrow-ga, lacagaha jeebka, ama lacag-bixinnada iibiyaha ee sugaya waa in marka hore la dhammeeyaa. Codsi helid, sixid, ama nuqul la qaadan karo oo aan abka laga heli karin u dir privacy@celis.so. Waxaan xaqiijin doonnaa in codsaduhu leeyahay koontada ka hor inta aan xog la bixin ama la beddelin.

9. Asturnaanta carruurta

Celis looguma talagelin qof ka yar 18 sano. Si ula kac ah xog shakhsiyadeed ugama ururinno carruurta. Haddii aad rumaysan tahay in ilmo xog siiyay Celis, la xiriir privacy@celis.so.

10. Goobta, wareejinta, iyo xuquuqda sharciga

Celis waxaa loogu talagalay suuqa Soomaaliya. Goobta Celis waa gobol laga doorto liis, mana aha GPS-ka qalabka. Supabase iyo Google waxay xogta ku maamuli karaan meel ka baxsan Soomaaliya. Sharciga lagu maamulayo Celis, goobta lagu xallinayo khilaafaadka, iyo in sharciyo sida GDPR-ka Midowga Yurub ay khuseeyaan isticmaalayaasha qurbaha qaarkood waxay u baahan yihiin dib-u-eegis qareen aqoon leh. Haddii sharciga khuseeya ku siiyo xuquuq asturnaan dheeraad ah, la xiriir privacy@celis.so.

11. Isbeddellada siyaasaddan

Waxaan siyaasaddan cusboonaysiin karnaa marka adeegyada Celis, adeeg-bixiyeyaasha, ama waajibaadka sharcigu isbeddelaan. Waxaan beddeli doonnaa taariikhda cusboonaysiinta, siyaasadda hadda jirtana waxaan ku daabici doonnaa abka iyo celis.so/privacy. Isbeddellada waaweyn waa in la eego ka hor sii-deynta si siyaasadda iyo jawaabaha Badbaadada Xogta Google Play ay isugu mid ahaadaan.

12. Xiriir

Codsiyada asturnaanta, su'aalaha xogta koontada, ama arrimaha ammaan ee aad ka shakisan tahay u dir privacy@celis.so. Ku dar emailka ama lambarka telefoonka koontada ku xiran, laakiin waligaa ha dirin furaha sirta ama koodhka xaqiijinta hal-mar ah.
