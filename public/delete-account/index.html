Delete your Celis account / Tirtir koontadaada Celis

EnglishSoomaali
Last updated: 2026-08-30

This page explains how to delete your Celis account and what happens to your data. It applies to the Celis mobile app (com.celis.mobile), published by Celis Somalia.

This cannot be undone

Deletion begins immediately. There is no recovery window, and you will lose access to Celis.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Celis app and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Profile, then Privacy & Safety, then Delete Account.
  3. Verify your identity with your password or an SMS verification code.
  4. Type DELETE, then confirm on the final prompt.

Deletion starts immediately once you confirm. There is no recovery window and no waiting period.

Before you can delete

Resolve active orders, escrow, wallet payments, or pending payouts before deleting your account.

Deleted permanently

  • Your profile, contact details, business details, and push token
  • Listings without completed transactions and their stored images
  • Saved items, notifications, blocks, and disposable activity data

Retained without your live identity

  • Existing messages needed by the other conversation participant
  • Reviews and moderation reports used for marketplace safety
  • Completed orders, payouts, and audit records required for accounting or disputes

Retained records are kept only for the purposes listed above and are no longer linked to a live account identity. Fixed retention periods for these records are under legal review and will be published here once set.

If you cannot sign in

Email support@celis.so from the email address on the account, or include the phone number you sign in with. Use "Account deletion request" as the subject. We verify that you own the account before deleting anything, and we respond within 30 days.

Markii ugu dambeysay ee la cusboonaysiiyay: 2026-08-30

Boggan wuxuu sharraxayaa sida aad koontadaada Celis u tirtiri karto iyo waxa ku dhacaya xogtaada. Wuxuu khuseeyaa abka mobilada ee Celis (com.celis.mobile), oo ay daabacday Celis Somalia.

Dib looma celin karo

Tirtiriddu isla markiiba ayay bilaabmaysaa. Ma jiro waqti soo-kabasho, gelitaanka Celisna waad waayi doontaa.

Sida aad abka uga tirtirto koontada

  1. Fur abka Celis oo gal koontada aad tirtirayso.
  2. Aad Astaanta, ka dibna Asturnaanta & Badbaadada, ka dibna Tirtir Koontada.
  3. Xaqiiji aqoonsigaaga adigoo isticmaalaya furahaaga sirta ah ama koodh SMS ah.
  4. Qor DELETE, ka dibna xaqiiji weydiinta ugu dambeysa.

Tirtiriddu isla markiiba way bilaabmaysaa marka aad xaqiijiso. Ma jiro waqti soo-kabasho ama sugid.

Waxa aad marka hore u baahan tahay

Dhammaystir dalabyada, escrow-ga, lacagaha jeebka, ama payout-yada sugaya ka hor tirtiridda.

Si joogto ah loo tirtirayo

  • Astaantaada, xiriirkaaga, xogta ganacsiga, iyo calaamadda ogeysiisyada
  • Xayeysiisyada aan lahayn macaamil dhammaystiran iyo sawirradooda kaydsan
  • Waxyaabaha la kaydiyay, ogeysiisyada, xannibaadaha, iyo xogta dhaqdhaqaaqa ee aan loo baahnayn

La haynayo iyadoo aqoonsigaaga nool laga saaray

  • Farriimaha hore ee uu u baahan yahay qofka kale ee wada hadalka
  • Qiimaynta iyo cabashooyinka loo isticmaalo badbaadada suuqa
  • Dalabyada, lacag-bixinnada, iyo diiwaannada xisaabeed ee dhammaystiran

Diiwaannada la hayo waxaa loo hayaa oo keliya ujeeddooyinka kor ku xusan, mana ku xidhna aqoonsi koonto oo nool. Muddooyinka go'an ee haynta diiwaannadaas waxaa lagu jiraa dib u eegis sharci, waana lagu daabici doonaa boggan marka la go'aamiyo.

Haddii aadan geli karin

Email u dir support@celis.so adigoo isticmaalaya cinwaanka email-ka ee koontada, ama ku dar lambarka telefoonka aad ku gasho. Mawduuca ku qor "Codsi tirtiridda koontada". Waxaan xaqiijinaynaa inaad leedahay koontada ka hor inta aanan waxba tirtirin, waxaanan kaaga jawaabaynaa 30 maalmood gudahood.
